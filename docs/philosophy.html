🤝 Gotong Royong Manifesto

"Evaluasi dan gotong royong saling membahu untuk dunia bisnis Indonesia yang kompetitif dan sehat sebagai investasi untuk anak-cucu dimasa yang akan datang."

— PT Koneksi Jaringan Indonesia

Nilai-Nilai Kami

✅

Evaluasi

Continuous assessment & improvement

Bukan: Build and forget
Tapi: Build, evaluate, improve, share learnings
🤝

Gotong Royong

Mutual cooperation untuk kemajuan bersama

Bukan: Zero-sum competition
Tapi: Positive-sum collaboration
💪

Saling Membahu

Mutual support untuk collective success

Bukan: Survive alone
Tapi: Advance together
⚖️

Kompetitif & Sehat

Healthy competition yang strengthens all

Bukan: Destructive competition
Tapi: Competition that improves everyone
🌱

Investasi Anak Cucu

Building for generations

Bukan: Quarterly profits only
Tapi: Generational prosperity

Yang Kami Tolak

❌

Destructive Competition

  • Meruntuhkan ekosistem yang ada
  • Price wars yang merusak kualitas
  • Zero-sum thinking
  • Winner-take-all mentality
❌

Exploitation

  • User data selling
  • Dark patterns
  • Predatory pricing
  • Vendor lock-in
❌

Short-Termism

  • Quarterly profit over long-term health
  • Extract value and leave
  • No community investment
  • Ignore externalities

Public Accountability

Kami Berjanji:

  • 📊 Publish quarterly progress reports
  • 📚 Share learnings (successes AND failures)
  • 🔍 Maintain transparency in decisions
  • 👂 Listen to community feedback
  • 📈 Adjust based on impact metrics
  • 🌟 Weekly Wisenesday vision updates

Kami Ukur Sukses Bukan Hanya dari Profit:

Ecosystem Health

Collaboration instances, quality standards, community strength

Generational Readiness

Student adoption, university integration, next-gen skills

Cultural Shift

Collaboration normalized, ethical AI expected, gotong royong practiced

Bergabung dalam Gotong Royong

Mari bersama-sama membuktikan: gotong royong adalah business advantage, bukan weakness. Collaboration raises all boats.
