### The Rabu Ekstrim Methodology

**Weekly Cycle:**
```
SENIN-SELASA: Implement new initiatives
RABU: Evaluate + challenge (counter jenuh)
KAMIS-JUMAT: Apply improvements
SABTU-MINGGU: Ideate + plan next week
```

**Why It Works:**
- Counter-cyclical emotion management
- Structure beats motivation
- Weekly learning loops (52/year)
- Compound growth trajectory

**Philosophy:**
> "Alih-alih mengikuti ritme emosional, saya berinisiatif 
> membuatnya sebagai hari penuh tantangan."

## 🎓 WHAT WE LEARNED

### About Innovation

**Discovery:**
- Innovation doesn't require massive teams
- One person's Rabu Ekstrim → ecosystem architecture
- Deep thinking > many meetings
- Documentation while fresh preserves brilliance

**Lesson:**

> Document immediately. Share generously."
