# Gotong Royong Manifesto: Business Philosophy for Healthy Ecosystem

**Company:** PT Koneksi Jaringan Indonesia  
**Published:** October 2025  
**Status:** Core Operating Philosophy  
**Commitment:** Long-term, generational

---

## 🤝 OUR PHILOSOPHY

### Core Belief

> "Koneksi bukan hanya sebagai perusahaan yang membuka potensi opensource untuk dunia pengembangan yang lebih kompetitif, inovatif, masif dan sehat, tapi juga benar-benar menjadi inisiator pengembang dengan dukungan jaringan atau komunitas yang kuat, fundamental, persisten yang mendorong perusahaan lama atau besar lain untuk terus maju."

---

## 🎯 WHAT WE STAND FOR

### ✅ We Believe In

**1. Evaluasi (Continuous Assessment)**
```
Not just: Build and forget
But: Build, evaluate, improve, share learnings

Applied to:
- Our products (weekly evaluation)
- Our processes (transparent retrospectives)
- Our ecosystem (health monitoring)
- Our impact (measurable outcomes)
```

**2. Gotong Royong (Mutual Cooperation)**
```
Not: Zero-sum competition
But: Positive-sum collaboration

Means:
- Open source by default
- Share knowledge freely
- Support competitors (they make us better)
- Raise all boats (ecosystem health > market share)
```

**3. Saling Membahu (Mutual Support)**
```
Not: Survive alone
But: Advance together

Practice:
- Help others succeed
- Celebrate competitor wins
- Share resources
- Collaborative problem-solving
```

**4. Kompetitif & Sehat (Healthy Competition)**
```
Not: Destructive competition
But: Competition that improves everyone

How:
- Compete on quality, not price wars
- Innovation, not imitation
- Transparency, not secrets
- Mutual respect, not undermining
```

**5. Investasi Anak Cucu (Generational Investment)**
```
Not: Quarterly profits only
But: Generational prosperity

Focus:
- Sustainable practices
- Knowledge preservation
- Culture building
- Future readiness
```

---

## ❌ WHAT WE STAND AGAINST

### We Reject

**1. Destructive Competition:**
```
❌ Meruntuhkan ekosistem yang ada
❌ Price wars that hurt quality
❌ Zero-sum thinking
❌ Winner-take-all mentality
```

**2. Exploitation:**
```
❌ User data selling
❌ Dark patterns
❌ Predatory pricing
❌ Vendor lock-in
```

**3. Short-Termism:**
```
❌ Quarterly profit over long-term health
❌ Extract value and leave
❌ No investment in community
❌ Ignore externalities
```

**4. Closed Systems:**
```
❌ Proprietary for control
❌ Knowledge hoarding
❌ Community exclusion
❌ Barrier to entry
```
