- ✅ **Mengangkat** standar ecosystem

### Open Source Commitment

**Janji Kami:**
- ✅ MIT License (free forever)
- ✅ Self-hosted option (data ownership)
- ✅ Community governance
- ✅ Transparent development
- ✅ Long-term maintenance (5+ years minimum)

### Ethical AI

**Principles:**
- ✅ Privacy-first design
- ✅ Explicit consent required
- ✅ Open source algorithms
- ✅ User control always
- ✅ No data selling (ever)
- ✅ Transparent operation

**Warning:**
> "With great power comes great responsibility. AI can connect OR divide, empower OR exploit. We choose connection and empowerment."
